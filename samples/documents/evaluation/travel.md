# Travel Policy

This policy covers business trips inside and outside the country.

## Booking

Book flights and hotels through the travel portal at least two weeks ahead.
Economy class is standard for flights shorter than six hours.

## Daily Allowance

The daily meal allowance is forty euros for trips inside the country.
Trips abroad use the allowance table published by the finance team.

## Cancellations

Cancel a booking through the travel portal as soon as the plan changes.
Unused tickets can be credited for twelve months.

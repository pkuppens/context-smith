# Employee Handbook

This handbook describes how the company treats its staff.

## Leave Policy

Annual leave is 25 days per calendar year.

### Parental Leave

Employees qualify for parental leave after twelve months of employment.
Parental leave lasts up to 26 weeks and is paid at full salary.

### Sick Leave

Staff who are ill must report sick leave to their manager before 10:00 on the first day.

## Benefits

The company pays a monthly pension contribution of five percent of gross salary.
Full-time staff can choose a dental plan or a health plan once per year.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims within thirty days using the finance portal.
Train travel is reimbursed in second class only.
